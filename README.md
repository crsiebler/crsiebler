## ⚡ What Drives Me

I connect architecture with execution, turning complex problems into dependable systems. My work spans NASA ground infrastructure, streaming experiences at Angel Studios, financial services, and custom platforms for businesses.

I’m a **Software Engineer Principal at PNC Bank** and the owner of **[Phi Technology Solutions](https://phitechsolutions.com/)**, where I help businesses build thoughtful digital experiences. I also explore how AI, agent workflows, and developer tooling can make engineering more effective.

- **Architecture:** Full-stack applications, cloud-native platforms, and clear system boundaries.
- **Delivery:** Practical automation, reliable CI/CD, and testing that builds confidence in each release.
- **Intelligence:** AI-enabled engineering, MCP integrations, and retrieval systems grounded in real problems.
